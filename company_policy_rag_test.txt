EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK AND COMPANY POLICY

Company: Northstar Technologies Pvt. Ltd. Document ID: HR-POL-001
Version: 1.0 Effective date: 1 April 2026 Department owner: Human
Resources

NOTE: This is a fictional company policy document created for testing a
Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) application. It is not legal advice
or a real company’s policy.

1.  PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This handbook explains the workplace rules, employee benefits,
attendance expectations, leave procedures, information security
requirements, and internal processes at Northstar Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

The policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees. Contractors
and interns must follow the sections that apply to their engagement and
any additional terms in their agreements.

If a department-specific procedure conflicts with this handbook,
employees should contact Human Resources for clarification. Only an
authorized company policy update can change the rules in this document.

2.  WORKING HOURS

The standard working week is Monday through Friday.

-   Standard working hours: 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
-   Lunch break: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.
-   Employees are expected to complete eight working hours per day,
    excluding lunch.
-   Employees may request a flexible start time between 8:00 AM and
    10:00 AM with manager approval.
-   Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed
    team collaboration hours.

Some teams may follow different schedules because of customer support,
operations, or project requirements. The team manager must communicate
any approved schedule variation.

3.  ATTENDANCE AND PUNCTUALITY

Employees must record attendance using the company’s approved attendance
system.

Employees who expect to arrive late should inform their manager before
their scheduled start time. If an employee cannot attend work because of
an emergency, they should notify their manager as soon as reasonably
possible.

Repeated unexplained absence or inaccurate attendance records may be
referred to Human Resources for review.

Employees must not record attendance on behalf of another employee.

4.  LEAVE POLICY

4.1 Annual Leave

Eligible full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year.

Annual leave should normally be requested at least five working days in
advance. Approval depends on team coverage and project requirements.

Unused annual leave may be carried forward up to a maximum of five days
into the next calendar year. Any additional carry-forward or payout is
subject to the applicable employment agreement and company rules.

4.2 Sick Leave

Eligible employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.

Employees should inform their manager as early as possible when taking
sick leave. For an absence of more than three consecutive working days,
Human Resources may request appropriate supporting documentation,
subject to applicable law.

4.3 Emergency Leave

Employees may request emergency leave when an unexpected personal or
family situation requires immediate attention.

The employee should notify their manager as soon as possible. Human
Resources will review the leave category and applicable balance.

4.4 Public Holidays

The company publishes an annual holiday calendar. Employees should refer
to the latest calendar issued by Human Resources because holiday dates
may vary by office location.

5.  REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week, subject to role
suitability and manager approval.

Remote work requests should be submitted through the company’s HR portal
at least one working day in advance.

Employees working remotely must: 1. Use a secure internet connection. 2.
Protect company devices from unauthorized access. 3. Attend scheduled
meetings. 4. Keep their work calendar updated. 5. Follow the same
working-hour and information-security requirements as office-based
employees.

Remote work is not an automatic entitlement. A manager may require
office attendance for meetings, training, customer visits, or
operational needs.

6.  CODE OF CONDUCT

All employees are expected to behave professionally and respectfully.

Employees must: - Treat colleagues, customers, and business partners
with respect. - Avoid harassment, discrimination, bullying, and
intimidation. - Protect confidential company and customer information. -
Disclose potential conflicts of interest. - Use company property
responsibly. - Follow applicable laws and company policies.

Employees must not use company systems to engage in unlawful activities,
distribute malicious software, or access information without
authorization.

Concerns about misconduct should be reported to Human Resources or
through the designated reporting channel.

7.  INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Company information must be protected against unauthorized access, loss,
or disclosure.

7.1 Passwords

Employees must use unique passwords for company accounts. Passwords must
not be shared with colleagues or stored in unapproved plain-text
documents.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled wherever the company
provides it.

7.2 Company Devices

Employees must use approved devices and software for company work. Lost
or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT Helpdesk
immediately.

Employees must not disable endpoint protection, bypass security
controls, or install unapproved software.

7.3 Data Classification

Company information is classified into three categories:

-   Public: Information approved for public distribution.
-   Internal: Information intended for employees and approved business
    partners.
-   Confidential: Sensitive business, employee, financial, or customer
    information requiring restricted access.

Confidential information must only be shared with authorized recipients
through approved company channels.

7.4 Phishing and Suspicious Messages

Employees must not open suspicious attachments or follow unexpected
links. Suspected phishing messages should be reported to the IT Helpdesk
using the approved reporting process.

8.  EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable, approved business
expenses.

Examples include: - Approved business travel. - Customer meeting
expenses. - Pre-approved software or equipment purchases. - Other
expenses authorized by a department manager.

Employees must submit expense claims through the finance portal within
30 calendar days of the expense date.

Each claim must include an itemized receipt, business purpose, expense
date, and manager approval where required.

The Finance Department aims to process complete and approved claims
within 10 business days. Incomplete claims may be returned for
additional information.

Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

9.  TRAVEL POLICY

Business travel must receive manager approval before booking.

Employees should use the company’s approved travel booking process.
Economy class is the standard option for domestic flights unless an
exception is approved by a department head.

Hotel bookings should follow the accommodation limits communicated by
Finance for the destination.

Employees must retain receipts for eligible travel expenses and submit
their claims within 30 calendar days after the expense date.

10. PROBATION AND PERFORMANCE REVIEWS

New employees normally complete a probation period of six months, unless
their employment agreement specifies otherwise.

Managers should provide feedback during probation and conduct a formal
review before the probation period ends.

Regular employees receive a performance review once per year. Reviews
may consider: - Quality of work. - Achievement of agreed goals. -
Collaboration and communication. - Reliability and ownership. -
Professional development.

A performance review does not automatically guarantee a salary increase,
promotion, or bonus.

11. LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

Employees may request support for job-related training and
certifications.

The annual learning budget is up to INR 15,000 per eligible employee,
subject to manager approval and available department funds.

Training requests must include the course name, provider, cost,
relevance to the employee’s role, and expected completion date.

Employees must obtain written approval before purchasing a course.
Unapproved purchases are not guaranteed reimbursement.

12. IT SUPPORT

Employees should report technical problems through the IT Helpdesk
portal.

The IT Helpdesk operates Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM,
excluding company holidays.

Standard initial response targets: - Critical: Company-wide system
outage — 1 business hour. - High: Employee cannot access a key business
system — 4 business hours. - Normal: Software issue affecting regular
work — 1 business day. - Low: General request or minor configuration
change — 3 business days.

These are initial response targets, not guaranteed resolution times. The
IT team may update the employee if investigation takes longer.

For a suspected security incident, employees must use the urgent
security reporting channel rather than waiting for a normal support
response.

13. RESIGNATION AND EXIT PROCESS

Employees who resign should submit written notice to their manager and
Human Resources.

The standard notice period is 30 calendar days, unless the employment
agreement specifies a different period.

Before the final working day, employees must: 1. Complete the handover
of assigned work. 2. Return company equipment and access cards. 3.
Transfer relevant work documents to approved company storage. 4. Submit
outstanding expense claims. 5. Complete the exit interview if requested.

Final settlement is processed according to the employment agreement and
applicable law.

14. POLICY QUESTIONS AND EXCEPTIONS

Employees who need clarification should contact Human Resources at
hr@northstar.example.

Questions about passwords, devices, or suspicious messages should be
sent to it-helpdesk@northstar.example.

Questions about reimbursement should be sent to
finance@northstar.example.

Exceptions to this handbook require written approval from the relevant
department head and Human Resources. An informal verbal statement does
not change the written policy.
