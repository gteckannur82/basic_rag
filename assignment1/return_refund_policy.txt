SHOPKART RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Return eligibility: A product can be returned if it is unused, in its original packaging, and returned with all accessories, tags and the invoice.

Return window: Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Refund processing time: Refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days after the returned product passes the quality check.

Replacement policy: A replacement is offered for defective or wrong items if the request is made within 7 days of delivery. If the replacement item is out of stock, ShopKart gives a full refund instead.

Damaged product procedure: If a product arrives damaged, report it within 48 hours of delivery under My Orders and upload photos or an unboxing video. ShopKart arranges a free pickup and offers a replacement or a full refund.

Non-returnable products: The following cannot be returned: earbuds and other in-ear audio products once the seal is opened, innerwear, gift cards, software and digital products, and any product with a missing or tampered serial number.
